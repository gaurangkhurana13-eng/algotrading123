API Documentation | LSE Data 3.0

The LSE Data 3.0 API is one HTTPS service: every call is a GET under /v3 with the key in the X-API-Key header. It serves eleven markets, US, UK and Canadian stocks, forex, crypto, futures, indices, commodities and US options, with CME Globex and Nasdaq TotalView on paid plans: candles at fifteen timeframes from tick to monthly, trades, quotes, the Level 2 book and Level 3 orders, options chains and flow with Greeks, macro observations, releases and bond yields by country, and nineteen reference collections. Exports are CSV or Parquet, streamed at once or prepared as a job with a SHA-256 checksum and resumable download. This page documents every call with its parameters, real answers and every refusal code.
